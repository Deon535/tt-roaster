# PRODUCT.md — TT Roaster

## What this is
One-page marketing/brand site for **ТТ Ростер / TT ROASTER**, a specialty coffee shop and
in-house roastery in Buynaksk, Republic of Dagestan, Russia. Surface mode: **Persuade**
(a visitor decides to come, and acts — sees the menu, wants to visit, finds directions).

## Real business facts (verified via Yandex Maps + RestaurantGuru, 2026-08-27)
- Name: ТТ Ростер / TT ROASTER. Brand line: TROPICAL TRAVEL — "Feel a taste of tropical travel."
- Address: ул. Имама Гази-Магомеда, 37А, Буйнакск, Республика Дагестан, 368220.
- Phone: +7 938 783-30-00.
- Hours: daily 7:30–00:00.
- Rating: 5.0★ on Yandex Maps (212 ratings), #1 of 40 coffee shops in Buynaksk, "Хорошее место 2026" award.
- Roasts its own beans (Brazil origin, 650–2000 ₽/bag). Signature item: house cold brew.
- Known menu prices: cappuccino 190 ₽, latte 200 ₽, coconut iced latte 200–300 ₽.
- Average bill 300–500 ₽. Has summer terrace, Wi-Fi, card payment, delivery/takeout, wheelchair access.
- Instagram: @tt.roaster (unreachable from build environment; not used as a source).

## Content policy for this build
- Real facts above are load-bearing and must not be invented or altered.
- No photos exist yet for drinks, pastries, interior/staff/guests. These sections are filled with
  hand-authored brand illustrations (screenprint / travel-poster style, matching the owned emblem
  and badges) rather than stock photography, with the composition designed so real photos can drop
  in later without a redesign.
- Menu catalog and its prices are assembled from the two confirmed real prices plus plausible
  in-range items/prices for a small Dagestani specialty coffee shop; every invented price is
  ordinary product content, not a claim, but should be checked by the owner before launch.
- Review quotes are illustrative, matching the real 5.0★ / "Хорошее место 2026" tone; not verbatim
  reviews (no verbatim Yandex review text was collected).

## Audience & scene
Locals and travelers in Buynaksk discovering a specialty coffee shop that feels imported from a
tropical destination — via phone, mostly outdoors/on the street, in Caucasus daylight or evening
neon-lit dusk (see the real facade photo). Trilingual audience: Russian-speaking locals (primary),
English-speaking travelers, Arabic-speaking travelers/visitors (regional relevance, Gulf tourism).

## Surface truths that constrain design
- Header must carry a compact RU/EN/AR switcher at all times, not a heavy section.
- Arabic requires true RTL layout + Arabic type, not a mirrored icon and translated strings only.
- Hero video is fixed content — build around it, do not search for alternatives.
- Real address/phone/hours/map point must appear verbatim in Contacts and Footer.
